Standard references follow the Singapore Standards on Auditing (SSAs, Singapore's adoption of the ISAs) and Singapore Financial Reporting Standards (International) — SFRS(I), which are identical to IFRS Accounting Standards. Paragraph summaries are paraphrased to help you navigate; always read the authoritative text before concluding. Check which versions apply to the period under audit — for example, the revised fraud standard, and SFRS(I) 18, which replaces SFRS(I) 1-1 for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027. Automated tests flag items for auditor judgement; they are not conclusions. Data is saved to your team's Supabase project and shared with signed-in team members.
